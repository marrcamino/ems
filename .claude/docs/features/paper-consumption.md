# Paper consumption

How the office records the bond paper it uses, and what EMS does with it.
Everything here was told by the owner on 23 November 2026, from the office's
flowchart of the paper process and from the form kept in their own office
(Unit 1), unless a line says otherwise. The office's paper form was shown on
24 November 2026 (see "The office's paper form").

## How the office handles paper today

1. GSU receives the bond paper on a PO and hands each office a stack of
   reams (see "How GSU gets the paper"). The owner expects GSU to record this
   hand-over on an RIS, as it does for other supplies, but has not confirmed
   it.
2. Each office keeps a form beside its stack. When someone opens a new ream,
   they write it on the form, with the size of the paper.
3. The form keeps a running total for the month, kept up by erasing and
   rewriting it, for example "1 A4 and 1 long".
4. At the end of the month, the office prints its monthly consumption and
   submits it to GSU. Whether the printed form is titled "monthly" is still to
   be checked by the owner.
5. GSU receives the printed form, and EMS must show that GSU has received
   it. The office cannot change a form it has submitted (owner, 25 November
   2026). GSU receives the submitted reports on a page of its own, apart
   from the Paper page on which every office, GSU included, records and
   submits its own paper. That page lists every office, GSU included, and
   only GSU and the system administrator can open it. The office calls the
   submitted document a report, never a "form" (owner, 25 November 2026).
   GSU does not receive its own report: submitting it marks it received at
   once, and the page says so on GSU's row (owner, 25 November 2026).
   GSU's Submit dialog says so too, in a note titled "Received
   automatically" with an "Okay, got it" button that hides it; the note comes back six months later, in case GSU has
   forgotten. There is no "Return to office" step (owner, 25 November 2026).
   An office can still edit a submitted report until GSU receives it, for
   example after submitting by accident and before printing, with no extra
   step to take it back; a received report is locked (owner, 25 November
   2026). GSU's own report, received the moment it is submitted, is changed
   by reopening it on its row of the submitted reports page; it is received
   again when GSU submits it again (owner, 25 November 2026). Built the same
   day (see "What EMS does").

## The office's paper form

Shown by the owner on 24 November 2026: the ICT Unit's form for October 2026,
titled "Bond Paper Consumption CY 2026", under the DENR letterhead. It is the
office's own form, not a COA form, so the owner can change it to fit EMS.

- **Header:** the month, then the office's division, section, and unit, each
  on its own line.
- **Columns:** the date, the number of reams in one column per size, who
  received the paper, and their signature. This form has two size columns, A4
  and Legal.
- **Four summary rows before the entries:**
  1. Balance forwarded: the reams left at the end of the previous month.
  2. New: the reams the office received during the month.
  3. Consumed: the reams opened during the month.
  4. Balance: the reams left at the end of the month.
- **Entries:** one line each time someone takes paper, with the date, the
  reams of each size, the name of the person who received it, and their
  signature. On the example, each line has one ream of one size.

Following the form in EMS, one topic at a time:

1. A date on the distribution, so its reams land in the right month's
   "New". Decided 24 November 2026, and changed the same day: "New" now
   counts GSU's hand-overs (see "What EMS does for it").
2. The balance forwarded for an office's first month in EMS, the paper it
   had before EMS. Decided 24 November 2026 (see "What EMS does").
3. Each entry as a line of the form: the date, the reams of each size, and
   who received the paper. Decided 24 November 2026 (see "What EMS does").
4. Whether an office can record more reams than it has. Decided 24 November
   2026: no (see "What EMS does").
5. Which sizes the form shows; this one has only A4 and Legal. Set aside on
   24 November 2026: the form is the office's own, not a COA form, so the
   owner can change it to fit EMS. EMS keeps its four sizes.
6. The print layout: the division, section, and unit, "Prepared by" and
   "Noted by", and the signature column. The layout waits (owner, 24 November
   2026); "Prepared by" and "Noted by" are built (see "What EMS does"), and
   the signature column is still to be asked.
- **Footer:** "Prepared by" the chief of the unit, and "Noted by" the chief of
  the section, each with name and position.

## The flowchart

The office's flowchart has two kinds of user.

- **An ordinary office** logs in, enters its monthly paper consumption,
  submits it, and views or prints its summary.
- **GSU** does the same for its own paper, and has three more jobs:
  1. it enters the bond paper it procured;
  2. it gathers the submitted consumption of every office and reviews it,
     which means checking the paper procured against what each office used;
  3. it submits the gathered figures to the EMS focal person, who keeps the
     combined data of the four EMS data features. The EMS focal person is the
     chief of the owner's office (Unit 1). How often the focal person makes
     the report is still to be asked; the owner's guess is once a year.
     This is a separate report from the offices' monthly reports: GSU
     consolidates every office's data into it, and once GSU submits it to
     the focal person, none of the data in it can change or be added to
     (owner, 25 November 2026).

## What EMS does

- A staff member enters each line of the form as someone takes paper: the
  date, the reams of each size (one line can hold A4 and Legal together), and
  who received it. "Received by" is picked from the office's own staff,
  starting on the person typing, or typed for someone not on the list. It is
  stored as the person's name on that date, as on the fuel forms, so a later
  name change does not rewrite a filed form. Who typed the line is kept too,
  but not shown. The signature is only on the printed form. Agreed by the
  owner on 24 November 2026, replacing a first build where an entry was one
  size and showed who entered it.
- At the end of the month, the office submits the month. Its entries are then
  marked complete and combine into one monthly report, the totals for each
  paper size, which is printed and given to GSU.
- A month can be submitted only once it is over, from the 1st of the next
  month: a submitted month is locked, so paper taken in its last days would
  otherwise have nowhere to be recorded, and its Balance is final only then.
  Until then Submit shows as unavailable, with "You can only submit after this
  month." (the owner's wording, 25 November 2026) under it, one line. Agreed
  by the owner on 25 November 2026. Until GSU receives the report, the
  office can still edit the month as it is; once GSU
  has received it, it cannot change, and reopening it is for the system
  administrator, not built yet (owner, 25 November 2026, replacing the rule
  that a submitted month has no undo; see "How the office handles paper
  today", step 5).
- While a month is open, an entry can be edited or removed, the same as
  erasing a line on the form. Once the month is submitted, nothing in it can
  change once GSU has received it (agreed by the owner, 23 November 2026;
  editing until GSU receives it added 25 November 2026).
- GSU receives the printed reports on the Submitted Reports page
  (`/paper/submitted`, key `paper:receive`), one month at a time, one row per
  office with its status, the reams of each size it used, and when it was
  submitted and received. Receive asks for the date the printed report
  arrived, from the submit date to today, and shows the office's totals so
  GSU can check them against the paper. GSU's own row shows "GSU's own
  report" and a Reopen button, which opens the month again to be submitted
  again. A received month locks every earlier month of the office too, since
  its balance forwarded counts them, and so do GSU's hand-overs dated in it
  or before it. Rapid build of 25 November 2026, for the owner to check.
- An office records and submits only its own paper. Seeing every office's is
  a separate permission, and it changes nothing (decided in the rapid build of
  23 November 2026, for the owner to check).
- Each month shows the four summary rows of the office's form, per size.
  "New" is the reams GSU handed to the office during the month (see "What
  EMS does for it"). "Consumed" is the month's entries. "Balance forwarded" is
  what was left from all the months before, not counting the current one, and
  "Balance" is balance forwarded + new − consumed (told by the owner,
  24 November 2026).
- The office's first month in EMS starts from the paper it already had: the
  office types it once, per size, as that month's balance forwarded, copied
  from the Balance row of its last paper form. It goes on a month with nothing
  recorded before it, can be corrected until that month is submitted, and no
  entry can go in a month before it. From then on EMS carries the balance
  forward by itself, counting only what is dated from the first month on, so
  paper already in the typed balance is never counted twice. Agreed by the
  owner on 24 November 2026; it answers MPP-144.
- An office cannot record more reams than it has: a line that would take a
  size's balance below zero, in its month or any later month, is refused,
  and a month whose balance is below zero cannot be submitted. A negative
  balance is always a mistake, a wrong number or paper GSU has not recorded
  yet; it can still appear after GSU edits a PO, and then shows red until the
  office sorts it out with GSU. So an office's first month needs its balance
  forwarded, or a PO from GSU, before anything can be recorded. Agreed by the
  owner on 24 November 2026.
- Submitting a month asks for the form's "Prepared by" and "Noted by", both
  required, picked from everyone still working here and stored as the
  person's name on the month's last day, as on the fuel forms. Each starts on
  the office's choice from its last submitted month. A Print button takes the
  place of Submit once the month is submitted, since there is no form to print
  before that; the printed form itself is not built yet. Asked for by the
  owner on 24 November 2026.
- Paper sizes are a fixed list written into EMS itself, never typed on each
  entry: A4 (210 × 297 mm), Short (216 × 279 mm), Long (216 × 330 mm), and
  Legal (216 × 356 mm), shown in millimetres (owner, 24 November 2026). The owner chose a fixed list over one that GSU
  manages, because these are the only sizes offices print documents on. A new
  size therefore needs a change to EMS, not a screen. A3 was on the list at
  first; the owner removed it on 24 November 2026, since offices do not print
  documents on it.

## How GSU gets the paper

Told by the owner on 24 November 2026, after asking the procurement office.

1. The procurement office starts the purchase and handles it up to the
   Purchase Order (PO).
2. The supplier signs the PO, and it is submitted to COA. COA forwards the PO
   to GSU.
3. GSU distributes the PO's paper to the offices, and to GSU itself if it
   needs some.

So GSU's part starts with the PO it receives from COA, much like a fuel PO,
whose liters GSU splits among the offices. Whether each distribution is
written on an RIS is still to be asked (MPP-143).

Told by the owner on 24 November 2026, from GSU:

- GSU sometimes keeps bond paper in its own stock that is already allotted to
  a particular office, for example when that office has no room to store it.
- Each office's share of a PO is its own: the office asked for it on its own
  PR, against its own budget, as with fuel. GSU, an office itself, does not
  decide how many reams an office gets. Paper an office leaves with GSU stays
  that office's: GSU only keeps it in the stock room and never gives it to
  another office. (Owner, 24 November 2026, correcting an earlier note that
  held paper could go to another office.)
- The PO's "Stock/Property No." column is to be used for the paper.
- The Project Procurement Management Plan (PPMP) is one of the first
  documents for buying supplies. Each office prepares one for the year, with
  what it plans to buy, the quantity, the estimated cost, and when; the
  PPMPs are gathered into the Annual Procurement Plan (APP), and common
  supplies such as bond paper also into the APP-CSE for PS-DBM (from a web
  search, 24 November 2026, not from the office). How the office uses its
  PPMP for paper is still to be seen.

This changes the decision of 24 November 2026 that a PO's paper is given out
all at once: being allocated to an office and being handed to it can now
happen on different days. The topics it raises, one at a time:

1. Allocated versus handed over. Built 24 November 2026 as a rapid build
   (see "What EMS does for it"). EMS counts paper in an office's "New" on the
   day it reaches the office; whether the office's form counts it then, or on
   the day the PO allocates it, is asked of GSU in MPP-145.
2. Giving paper GSU holds for one office to another office. Closed: it is
   never done, since the paper is that office's own.
3. A GSU view of the paper it holds for each office. Open; the PO card shows
   it per PO for now.
4. The PO's "Stock/Property No." on each paper size. Built 24 November 2026
   as a rapid build: GSU enters it on the Stock room step for each size with
   paper in the stock room, and it is required then. The owner's guess is
   that GSU writes a stock number only for a supply it stocks in the stock
   room (fuel POs have none); MPP-146 asks GSU.
5. Whether EMS records each office's PPMP. Open, probably later.

What EMS does for it (rapid build of 24 November 2026, for the owner to
check):

- GSU types the PO as printed: its number, date, supplier, and the reams of
  each paper size, plus remarks if it needs any. The supplier comes from the
  same list as the fuel POs, on its own Suppliers page. The owner first
  removed the supplier on 24 November 2026, because the list was then fuel's
  own; it came back the same day, once suppliers got their own page. A PO
  typed in between has no supplier until it is edited.
- GSU then records the reams of each size allocated to each office, GSU
  included. These must add up to the PO exactly: every ream is allocated.
  The owner made this strict on 24 November 2026, replacing the first build,
  which let a PO be allocated in parts.
- Paper POs use the same permissions as the fuel POs.
- GSU may keep part of an office's share and hand it over later. Each
  hand-over is recorded with its date and the reams of each size, and only
  hand-overs count in the office's "New", in the month of their date; the
  rest is held by GSU for that office. Adding a PO has a third step, "Stock
  room", after Distribution: it lists every office from Distribution, each
  with its own share of each size, and how many of those reams it leaves
  with GSU (never more than its own share), and the date the rest was handed
  over (from the PO date to today). Every office gets the part of its share
  it does not leave, on that date. Distribution starts with one empty office
  row.
  Later hand-overs of what is kept are recorded from the PO card's Hand over
  button, never more than GSU holds for the office. The owner asked for this
  step instead of a "Hand it all over now" switch, 24 November 2026. An edit
  cannot make an office's share smaller than what it was already handed, and
  a hand-over cannot be dated in a month the office has already submitted,
  since a submitted form never changes.
  Rapid build of 24 November 2026, replacing the single "Date distributed"
  built earlier that day.
- A distribution has no RIS number yet (MPP-143).

The Paper stock page built on 23 November 2026 started instead from the
Inspection and Acceptance Report and GSU's own stock. The owner found that
this is not the process, and it was replaced by the paper POs on
24 November 2026.
