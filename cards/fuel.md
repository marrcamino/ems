# Fuel

Assets, purchase orders, and later the withdrawal slips. Suppliers have their own page, `/suppliers`, shared with the paper POs. The only area with an approval step: GSU checks and approves each request before the fuel data goes on to the GHG Focal Person (process in `.claude/docs/project-brief.md`, "The process at a glance").

- **Approve, Complete, Edit, Cancel, and Close PO** (rapid build, 23 November 2026, f4b35e4). A cancelled row keeps its Edit button, which shows why it cannot be edited, as on a closed PO. Who may do what is one rule, `transaction-actions.ts`, which the row and the server both use: `fuel:approve` marks a slip approved (Waiting for approval → Out); `fuel:complete` completes, and edits and cancels at any step; `fuel:record` alone edits and cancels only before GSU has acted (a withdrawal still Waiting for approval, a trip still Out). A Complete or Incomplete one is never cancelled, and a withdrawal on a closed PO is locked. The GSU role template holds both new keys. Complete (`complete-dialog.svelte`, `completeTransaction` in `issuance.server.ts`): on a liter-based PO EMS records the liters requested at the PO's unit cost; on a price-based PO GSU types the unit price and the total amount, and EMS works out the liters; the trip ticket's lines after the trip are typed there too, and a withdrawal without its tank lines becomes Incomplete (point 9). A slip that is back takes its real amount off the PO. Edit reopens the same form filled in (`GET /fuel/transactions/[id]`, `form.load`), keeping the file date and every number; a withdrawal that gains a trip ticket gets its DTT number then. New columns on `fuel_issuance` (`tmp/fuel-issuance-complete-fields.sql`) and `purchase_order.closed_at` (`tmp/purchase-order-closed-at.sql`; a PO is closed while it holds a date), both run by the owner on 23 November 2026. Close PO opens the check in `close-po-dialog.svelte` (`po-close.ts`, shared with the server): a slip not back or money left stops it, and an Incomplete slip is a warning with "Close anyway". A closed PO takes no new withdrawal, cannot be edited or deleted, and shows Reopen in the Closed list.
