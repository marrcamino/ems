## Gotchas
- The Fuel page (`/fuel`) shows made-up rows across the 8 offices from `sample-withdrawals.ts` (branch `feature/fuel-issuance`, 26 October 2026), to show GSU and the GHG Focal Person an Office column in place of the office prefix on the control number. Since 27 October 2026 there are 105, from December 2025 to 13 November 2026, so the numbers start over in January: 6 trips only, 3 reimbursed trips, 3 cancelled, 3 Incomplete, and about 15% of the pump prices with centavos. `sampleWithdrawals()` returns only the rows filed up to today, and turns the newest into 2 Waiting for approval and 2 Out. The quarterly POs give every office its allocation again, so the balances go back up. Its table follows layout 1b of the Claude Design "Withdrawals Table" (built 27 October 2026; its year range picker of layout 2a added 29 October 2026 and removed 31 October 2026): 8 columns with the details in a smaller line under the main value, numbers without the year (`short-numbers.ts`), the control number in bold as one count across every office with a small badge for the office's own count for the year (both start over in January), the fuel badge inside the Withdrawn cell (diesel teal, gasoline red-orange), and a slip that is not back yet shows "—" with "Requested X L", but keeps its estimated balance. A Status column after Slip (added 27 October 2026, not in the design) shows the five statuses in their colours from `withdrawal-status.ts`, without icons, because the words and colours already say it and the gas pump for Out read as "fuel" (removed 29 October 2026); it replaced the design's "Out" badge in the Slip cell. A cancelled slip takes nothing off the balance, and a trip shows "—" as its balance. The last column, pinned to the right edge, holds an Edit button and a menu (Print, Copy control number, View history); none of them does anything yet. The search box (`withdrawal-search.ts`, 28 October 2026) looks at the asset, the remarks, the driver, and the CN, WS, RIS, and DTT numbers, never at litres or pesos. Every word must match, a number matches only as the list shows it (without the year), and "WS 10-093" limits the number to one form. Beside the search are two filters, Office and Fuel (`filter-combobox.svelte`, which ticks several options, with a Clear at the bottom of its list; since 31 October 2026 its button reads like "Office: All ▾"). Fuel with one office makes the estimated balance read like GSU's logbook, one fuel at a time. The Year filter and the Clear all button were removed on 31 October 2026, because the page will show only the transactions of open POs, and each filter already has its own Clear; the year picker is kept in `tmp/archive/year-range-picker.svelte`. At the top, the heading "Fuel withdrawals" (1 November 2026) has a line under it naming the open PO in use and since when, or the POs that mix and since when, worked out by `po-in-use.ts` with the rule of point 10.3; the made-up withdrawals got PO numbers for it, and only the newest made-up PO is open. Below the heading, status tabs (the shared `RadioTabs`, `src/lib/components/radio-tabs.svelte`, 31 October 2026, replacing the Status filter of 29 October) are radio buttons styled as tabs with an underline that slides and stretches to the picked tab: All, For approval, Out, Incomplete, Complete, Cancelled, each with a count of the rows that the search and the filters leave. They help GSU find the slips that wait on it. The box is the shared `SearchInput` (`src/lib/components/search-input.svelte`): Escape or its × empties it, and the × puts the cursor back in the box. The search and the filters hide rows instead of taking them out of the table, because building rows again made typing slow (28 October 2026). Each sortable header shows the shared `SortIcon` (`src/lib/components/sort-icon.svelte`), and the column's `meta.sortIcon` in `withdrawal-columns.ts` picks its type: calendar for the date, A–Z for words, 0–1 for the control number, bars for the litres, and the plain arrow for Status (29 October 2026).
- The PO page (layout 2a of the Claude Design "Purchase Orders Page") shows real POs, but slips not back and the Closed list are made up in `sample-purchase-orders.ts`, and "used" is ₱0, until slips and closing exist. Editing and deleting a PO have no checks yet (TODOs in `updatePurchaseOrder` and `deletePurchaseOrder`).
- Liters and pesos are counted in whole hundredths (`po-math.ts`), so allocations add up exactly in liters. Their pesos are rounded one by one and can be a centavo off, until MPP-118 limits the PO form to whole liters (decided 22 October 2026).
- Suppliers are active or **archived**; every other lookup table is active/inactive.
- "Allocation" is the word, never "share" or "allotment" (decided 13 October 2026).
- The pages are shared by GSU and every office: `fuel:view` opens `/fuel`, while Assets and Purchase Orders have their own keys, `assets` and `purchase_orders`.
